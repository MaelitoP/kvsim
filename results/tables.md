# Benchmark tables

Generated by `benchmarks/analyze.py` from the result files of this directory; do not edit. **Measured** values come from the engines and the load client: the median over the runs of a cell. **Simulated** counters and **estimated** times come from a kvsim replay of the same workload with the engine's cache size and settings; the replay is regenerated, not stored. A dash is a value that is not published, not defined, such as a hit rate when nothing was looked up, or missing from one of the runs.
